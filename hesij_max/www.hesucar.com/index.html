About Hesij Auto

China's First-Batch Auto-Export Enterprise

Hesij Auto is a subsidiary of Hesij Group, specializing in automobile export which maintains deep cooperation with over 30 automotive brands, including BYD, Changan, Geely, Leopard, ROX, Nissan, Zeeker, Li Auto and more. Our products covers gasoline, hybrid, plug-in hybrid, full EV version in sedans, SUVs, pickups and heavy‑duty trucks.

As one of China’s first‑batch auto‑export enterprises, Hesij Auto has delivered vehicles to more than 70 countries and regions across the Middle East, Africa, South America and Europe, with cumulative export volume exceeding 50,000 units.

Hesij Auto operates four overseas branches located in Dubai, Ghana, Ethiopia, and Djibouti, built up localized service network to provide overseas customers efficient and convenient one‑stop comprehensive automotive solutions. Committed to provide high‑quality automotive products and services, Hesij Auto strives to win global customers’ recognition for China‑made smart vehicles.
